Nestora Privacy Policy

Effective date: Effective on publication

Nestora is operated by M Mudasir ("Nestora," "we," or "us"). We operate Nestora from 10th Cross Dodenakuddi, Whitefield, Bangalore, India. For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, contact support@mynestora.in.

Information we handle

Depending on how you use Nestora, we handle account details such as your name, email address, optional phone number, and account role; property, room, and bed details; tenant invitations and assignments; rent and bill records; rent-proof images uploaded by tenants; complaints, vacate requests, announcements, community messages, and push-notification tokens; and account-deletion request status and timestamps.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide owner and tenant features, manage property and tenancy records, deliver notifications you authorize, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, and maintain and improve the service.

Service providers and sharing

Nestora uses Supabase for authentication, database, file storage, and server functions, and Expo Push Service to deliver push notifications. These providers process information on our behalf to operate the app. We do not store full payment-card details in Nestora. We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with service providers that support the app, with other users only when necessary for property, tenancy, rent, bill, complaint, announcement, community, or notification features, or when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and property information only while it is needed to provide Nestora and comply with legal, security, dispute-resolution, and record-keeping obligations.

You can request account deletion from Account & Privacy in the app, or follow the instructions on our account deletion page. We verify each request and aim to complete approved requests within 45 days, although legally required or technically necessary retention may result in a longer period.

Owner accounts may be connected to tenant accounts and shared property, rent, bill, complaint, and tenancy records. When an account is deleted, we may delete or de-identify the user's personal account data while retaining or transferring shared records if they are still needed for property administration or applicable recordkeeping. We will explain any significant retention during the request review.

Your choices and security

You may review and change your app information where an edit feature is available, use device settings to control notification permission, and sign out of the app. We use Supabase access controls and database policies intended to restrict data to authorized users. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; contact us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

Children

Nestora is intended for property owners and tenants, not children. Do not create an account for a child or upload a child's personal information unless legally authorized.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as Nestora changes. We will update the effective date and provide notice of material changes where required.
