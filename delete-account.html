Request deletion from inside the app

If you can sign in to Nestora, open Account & Privacy, choose Request account deletion, and confirm. This records a deletion request for review. It does not immediately erase your account or shared property records.

Request deletion without signing in

If you cannot access your account or no longer have the app, email support@mynestora.in from the email address registered to your Nestora account. Use the subject Nestora account deletion request.

Do not send your password, verification codes, payment credentials, or sensitive identity documents. We may ask for limited information needed to verify that you control the account.

What happens next

We will verify the request, review the account and its linked data, and contact you if we need more information. Owner accounts may be linked to tenant accounts and shared property, rent, bill, complaint, and tenancy records. Shared records may be retained or transferred where needed for ongoing property administration or applicable recordkeeping.

We aim to complete approved requests within 45 days. If an applicable law or security, fraud, dispute, or recordkeeping requirement requires us to retain information, we will retain only the information needed and delete or de-identify it when the requirement no longer applies.
